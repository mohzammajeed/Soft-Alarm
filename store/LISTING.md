## Full description (max 4000 characters)
Wake up the way you deserve: with a voice, not a beep.

SoftAlarm is an alarm clock that replaces harsh beeping with human voices: a caring mother, a proud father, a teasing sister, a motivating coach and more. Start your morning with a smile instead of a startle.

GENTLE BY DESIGN
- The alarm starts quietly and rises slowly, so you are never jolted awake.
- Voices instead of loud tunes.
- Works with the screen off and on the lock screen, and comes back after a restart.

12 VOICES IN 4 LANGUAGES
- Mother, Father, Sister, Brother, Grandmother, Husband, Wife, Motivational Coach, Boss, Employee, Azan and Child / Little Baby.
- Every voice speaks English, Punjabi, Urdu and Hindi.
- Some voices are sweet, some are playful, some are firm: pick the one that gets you out of bed.
- Add your own: choose any audio file from your phone.

ALSO INCLUDED
- Unlimited alarms, repeat days, labels.
- Timer and stopwatch with laps.
- The phone's own alarm tone and a soft SoftAlarm tune.

PRIVATE
- No ads and no tracking. Your alarms stay on your phone.
- You can delete your online account and data inside the app.
