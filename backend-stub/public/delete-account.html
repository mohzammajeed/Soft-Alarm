Delete your SoftAlarm account

{{PUBLISHER}} · last updated {{UPDATED}}

Option 1: inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open SoftAlarm.
  2. On the Alarm tab, tap Account & restore.
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

Option 2: by email

Email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} from the address you signed in with (or tell us your Account ID, shown in the app's Account screen) and ask us to delete your account. We aim to finish within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account (guest or Google/email) and its email address.
  • Your user record: account ID, dates, app version, and Pro status.

What is kept

  • Payment records are kept without any link to you because accounting and tax rules require it.
  • Polar (the payment provider) keeps its own records of your purchase under its own policy.

Before you delete

Your Pro unlock belongs to your account, so it is removed when the account is deleted and cannot be restored. Your alarms are stored on your phone and are not affected. Uninstalling the app also removes them.

See also the Privacy Policy.
